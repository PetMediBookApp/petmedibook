Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you download.

Is PetMediBook free?

Yes — PetMediBook is free to download and use. Some features may become part of an optional premium plan in the future.

Do I need an account?

No account or sign-up is required. Your pet's data stays on your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes, fully offline. Backup to your own Google Drive is optional.

What can I track?

Vaccinations with dates, weight over time, vet documents, reminders, and everyday memories — for one or multiple pets.

Can I export my pet's records?

Yes. You can export a clean medical extract as a PDF to share with your vet.

Is it medical advice?

No. PetMediBook is a record-keeping and memory tool, not a medical or diagnostic service.

Private by design.

PetMediBook is a record-keeping and memory tool — not medical advice. Your data stays on your device; sync to your own Google Drive is optional. No account required.
