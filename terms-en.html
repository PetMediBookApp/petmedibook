2. Service Description

PetMediBook is a mobile application developed for the Android platform, enabling pet owners to:

  • Maintain health records of their pets (vaccinations, weight, documents)
  • Manage veterinary contacts and appointments
  • Set health-related reminders
  • Back up data to their own Google Drive (optional)

Information stored in or suggested by the application does not constitute medical advice. In case of any pet health issue, ALWAYS consult a qualified veterinarian.
