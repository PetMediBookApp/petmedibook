Privacy Policy

PetMediBook Android Application
Effective from: May 22, 2026
Version: 1.0
In brief: PetMediBook stores your pet's health data only on your device. We, the developers, do not have access to your data. Optional backups are stored in a hidden, isolated folder in your own Google Drive. The application contains no advertisements, does not sell data, and does not employ user tracking.

Contact: petmedibookapp@gmail.com

The data controller does not appoint a Data Protection Officer (DPO), as the data processing activity does not reach the threshold specified in Article 37 of the GDPR. For privacy-related inquiries, please contact the data controller at the email address above.

2. What Data We Process

2.1. Pet health data (stored locally)

Data you record that PetMediBook stores on your device:

  • Pet basic data: name, species, breed, date of birth, sex, chip number, photograph
  • Health records: vaccination types, dates, veterinarian names, medication batch numbers
  • Weight history: dates and weight values
  • Medical documents: lab results, prescriptions, examination reports (photographs)
  • Veterinary contacts: name, clinic, phone number, email, address
  • Appointments: date, time, reason, reminder settings

2.2. User account and settings data

  • Language preference
  • Google account identifier (ONLY if you enable Google Drive backup)
  • Subscription status (Free or Premium)
  • Subscription transaction identifier received from Google Play Billing (NOT credit card data)

2.3. What we do NOT collect

  • Location data (GPS, IP-based location)
  • Advertising ID
  • Device usage analytics, telemetry, or behavioral patterns
  • Contacts, calendar, messages, or other phone data
  • Credit card data (handled exclusively by Google Play Billing, never accessible to us)

3. Legal Basis and Purpose of Processing

The legal basis for processing is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — performance of a contract (i.e., providing the PetMediBook service to you) — and where applicable Article 6(1)(a) — your explicit consent (particularly for Google Drive backup).

The purpose of processing is to:

  • Provide the PetMediBook service (pet health record keeping)
  • Execute Google Drive backup when you explicitly request it
  • Maintain and manage subscription status
  • Provide user support upon your explicit inquiry

4. Data Storage

4.1. Primary storage: Your device

All data you record is stored exclusively on your Android device, in a locally encrypted SQLite database (using Room ORM). Data is not transferred directly or indirectly to any other service provider, and the developer (Endre Kéri) has no access to it.

4.2. Optional storage: Your Google Drive

If you enable Google Drive backup in the Settings menu, the application backs up your data to a hidden, isolated app folder (drive.appfolder scope) within your Google account. This folder:

  • Is visible only to the PetMediBook application
  • Does NOT appear in your normal Google Drive web interface
  • Is tied only to your Google account; we do not have access
  • Is subject to Google's data processing terms (see: Google Privacy Policy)

5. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We DO NOT sell and DO NOT share your data with third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

We use the following technical data processors:

  • Google LLC (Google Play Billing) — subscription management. Google Play handles payment transactions. We never see your credit card data; we only receive an anonymous transaction identifier to verify subscription validity. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google LLC (Google Drive) — only with your explicit consent. Activated only if you enable backup. Data is then stored in your own Google Drive.

6. Data Retention

  • Pet data: until you uninstall the application from your device, or until you delete the data within the application
  • Drive backup: until you revoke PetMediBook's access in your Google account settings, or delete the Drive app folder
  • Subscription data: for the duration of the active subscription plus 6 months (for tax compliance per Google Play rules)

7. Your Rights under the GDPR

Under EU GDPR Regulation 2016/679, you have the following rights:

7.1. Right of access

You have the right to request information about what data we process about you. As data is stored exclusively on your device, in practice this means you can directly view your data within the application.

7.2. Right to rectification

You can modify any data within the application.

7.3. Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")

Uninstalling the application erases all local data. If you used Google Drive backup, you can manually delete the Drive folder in your Google account settings (Drive > Settings > Manage apps > PetMediBook > Disconnect).

7.4. Right to data portability

Premium subscribers can download their pet data in structured form using the in-app PDF Export feature.

7.5. Right to withdraw consent

You can withdraw your consent for Google Drive backup at any time: either in the application's Settings menu, or directly in your Google account settings.

7.6. Right to lodge a complaint

You may file a complaint with the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH):

  • Phone: +36 (1) 391-1400

8. Data Security

We employ the following measures to protect your data:

  • Data on your device is protected by the Android operating system's built-in encryption
  • Google Drive communication uses TLS/SSL encryption
  • The Google Drive folder is isolated in the appfolder scope, accessible only to PetMediBook
  • Subscription transactions are handled by Google Play Billing's PCI-DSS-compliant infrastructure

9. Children's Data

PetMediBook is not intended for children under the age of 16. The application is classified as "Everyone" in the Google Play Store age category, but we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you become aware that a user under 16 has used the application without parental consent, please contact us at the email address above, and we will arrange for the deletion of such data.

10. Cookies and Tracking

PetMediBook is a native Android application. We do not use cookies, web tracking, fingerprinting, or other identification techniques. The application contains no advertisements and uses no third-party analytics tools.

11. Changes to this Policy

If our data processing practices change, we will update this policy. Changes will be:

  • Communicated to you through in-app notification
  • Published on our Google Play Store listing
  • For material changes, we will request your renewed explicit consent

12. Jurisdiction and Applicable Law

This privacy policy is governed by Hungarian law and EU GDPR Regulation 2016/679. In the event of a dispute, the competent Hungarian court shall have jurisdiction. The parties shall first attempt to settle disputes amicably.
